# Customer Sales Intelligence

##  Project Overview

Customer Sales Intelligence is an end-to-end Data Analytics project focused on understanding customer purchasing behavior, customer value, retention, and revenue performance.

The project uses SQL for data analysis, Python and Pandas for customer-level analysis, and Power BI for interactive dashboard development.

---

## Business Objective

The main objectives of this project are:

- Understand the overall customer base
- Identify active and inactive customers
- Analyze customer purchasing frequency
- Identify high-value inactive customers for re-engagement
- Analyze revenue generated by different customer activity groups
- Compare one-time and repeat customer revenue
- Identify top customers by revenue
- Analyze customer revenue trends over time
- Support data-driven customer retention decisions

##  SQL Analysis

SQL was used to analyze customer purchasing behavior and revenue.

Examples of analysis performed:

- Top customers by revenue
- Customers with multiple orders
- Customer order frequency
- Last purchase date
- Customer revenue
- High-value inactive customers

Example:

```sql
SELECT
    c.customer_unique_id,
    MAX(o.order_purchase_timestamp) AS last_purchase_date,
    SUM(oi.price) AS total_revenue
FROM Orders o
JOIN Customers c
    ON o.customer_id = c.customer_id
JOIN Order_Items oi
    ON o.order_id = oi.order_id
GROUP BY
    c.customer_unique_id
HAVING
    MAX(o.order_purchase_timestamp) < DATE_SUB(
        (SELECT MAX(order_purchase_timestamp) FROM Orders),
        INTERVAL 6 MONTH
    )
    AND SUM(oi.price) >= 3000
ORDER BY total_revenue DESC
LIMIT 20;

##  Decision-Driven Analysis

This project is designed to move beyond reporting metrics and support practical business decisions.

The analysis helps answer questions such as:

### 1. Which customers need re-engagement?
Identify inactive customers, especially high-value inactive customers, so they can be prioritized for targeted retention campaigns.

### 2. Where is customer revenue concentrated?
Analyze revenue across customer activity groups to understand which customer segments contribute most to revenue.

### 3. Which customers contribute the most revenue?
Use Top 10 customer analysis to identify high-value customers and understand revenue concentration.

### 4. How does customer purchasing behavior affect revenue?
Compare one-time and repeat customers to understand differences in purchasing behavior and revenue contribution.

### 5. Which customer groups represent higher retention risk?
Analyze customer activity based on time since their last purchase to identify customers who may require different retention strategies.

### 6. How does customer revenue change over time?
Use the monthly customer revenue trend to identify changes in revenue patterns and periods requiring further investigation.
